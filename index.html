✕ The kiln reached temperature at dawn, Renata loaded the shelves before the studio opened.
✓ The kiln reached temperature at dawn, so Renata loaded the shelves before the studio opened.

Both halves are complete sentences, so a comma alone can’t join them. That’s a comma splice, and it’s the most common punctuation error on the test. Every question here comes with that explanation — plus a reason each of the other three choices is wrong.
