This is the kind of thing it drills

✕ The kiln reached temperature at dawn, Renata loaded the shelves before the studio opened.
✓ The kiln reached temperature at dawn, so Renata loaded the shelves before the studio opened.

Both halves are complete sentences, so a comma alone cannot join them. That is a comma splice, and it is the single most common punctuation error on the test. Every question here comes with that explanation — plus a reason each of the other three choices is wrong.
